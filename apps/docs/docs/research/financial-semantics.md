# Financial semantics and selection evidence

Recorded Apple FY2025 filings: 2024-09-28 10-K, 2024-12-28 10-Q, 2025-03-29 10-Q, 2025-06-28 10-Q, 2025-09-27 10-K. These are report dates in [Apple submissions](https://data.sec.gov/submissions/CIK0000320193.json), not calendar-frame labels. In [Apple company facts](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0000320193.json), Q2 revenue has six-month `start=2024-09-29, end=2025-03-29` and direct quarter `start=2024-12-29, end=2025-03-29`. Assets at 2025-03-29 have no start date and carry frame `CY2025Q1I`, despite being Apple fiscal Q2. The client's period engine therefore uses filing report dates and fact start/end dates; it does not equate `fy`, `fp`, or calendar frame with an economic period.

A direct fact with the exact start/end wins. For a balance field, an instant fact at the exact quarter end is required. Income and cash-flow duration fields use matching start/end dates. When a direct quarter is absent, an approved additive income or cash-flow concept may be computed as later fiscal-year-to-date minus prior fiscal-year-to-date, both with the same taxonomy tag and unit. No EPS, ratios, weighted-average shares, or instant facts are subtracted. Derived operands and exact decimal result are shown. A current annual report and prior annual report establish fiscal-year boundaries; absent metadata yields an explicit error.

Default revision policy ranks later periodic filings first for exact periods. `asFiled` prefers the accession of the original target-period filing. `asOf` excludes facts filed after that date. This is a deterministic client policy, not an SEC endorsement. Mixed filing sources are warned. Cross-currency comparison is not performed. Numeric tokens beyond the safe JS integer range remain decimal strings from the parser; numeric `values` become `null` with a precision warning while `details.exactValue` is preserved. `precision: 'string'` returns decimal strings in `values`.

The public [XBRL JSON API](https://www.sec.gov/search-filings/edgar-application-programming-interfaces) aggregates only standard taxonomy, entity-wide facts. Missing custom or dimensional line items stay missing. [SEC financial data sets](https://www.sec.gov/data-research/sec-markets-data/financial-statement-data-sets) have a different as-filed, rendered-face scope and their own extraction limitations.

Additional recorded examples: [Apple FY2023 10-K](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/320193/000032019323000106/aapl-20230930.htm) describes a 53-week year; fixture dates produce a 371-day period. [SAP 2025 20-F](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1000184/000110465926020058/sap-20251231x20f.htm) uses IFRS and EUR facts. [ICMB 2024 10-KT](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1578348/000095017025044673/icmb-20241231.htm) covers a six-month transition after a year-end change. Its June 2024 annual and December 2024 transition reports share a calendar year; `periodEnd` disambiguates them.

**Unverified / release blockers:** More changed-year-end cases, industry-specific mappings, and real amended values need fixtures before broad correctness claims.

Apple FY2025 operating cash-flow facts are year-to-date at each 10-Q and full-year at the 10-K. The Q2 standalone value is 23,952,000,000 USD from six-month 53,887,000,000 minus Q1 29,935,000,000. See the [recorded company facts](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0000320193.json). This derivation applies to approved cash-flow concepts only.

[BayFirst's 2025 10-K/A](https://www.sec.gov/Archives/edgar/data/1649739/000164973926000049/0001649739-26-000049.txt) is a recorded restatement. Net income for 2025 changed from -22,937,000 to -24,565,000 USD; assets at 2025-12-31 changed from 1,300,258,000 to 1,294,269,000 USD. These exact figures are in the [SEC company facts](https://data.sec.gov/api/xbrl/companyfacts/CIK0001649739.json) and compact fixtures. The `latest` policy chooses the amended values; `asFiled` and `asOf: '2026-03-27'` choose original values. The selector tries direct facts across all mapping aliases before attempting additive derivation. `asOf` filters fact filing dates; current submissions metadata supplies period boundaries, so it is not a historical API snapshot.
